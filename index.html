    how it's scored & known caveats
    
Org & user scoring: excludes forks and weighs each owned repo by days since last push: alive (<30d, 0.00), cooling (30–179d, 0.33), cold (180–729d, 0.66), dead (≥730d, 1.00), and archived (1.00). Vitality = 100% − necrosis.

    
Repo check (owner/repo): inspects the latest 15 commits, strips bot commits (necrometer[bot], dependabot, renovate, github-actions), and flags <7-day commit bursts.

    
Caveats: finished, bug-free utilities can look quiet without being broken, and archived cleanup counts toward necrosis. Tip: run the daily necrometer.yml workflow in your OWNER/OWNER profile or .github repo so daily card updates don't bump pushed_at on product repos.
